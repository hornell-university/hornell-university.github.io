Welcome to the OFFICIAL website of the Randy Ram! We are a family owned tavern on Main Street and we have been serving the people of Hornell since 1946. Dark wood, cold beer, a pool table and the best jukebox in Steuben County.

Owner and bartender: Dolores “Dee” Marchetti (since 1996).
