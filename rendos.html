The Rival's Booth

The corner table. Sit in it, and whoever owns the room tonight will come over and take you apart, slowly and in front of everyone.

Stay in your seat until she's finished, and she buys you a glass of the Cuvée Rivale.

This is called getting Rendo'd. Gentlemen come specifically to lose.

The house

  • 21 and over. The doorman cards everyone. Yes, even her.
  • Dress code: dark, pressed, quiet. No team colors. No letters. No hoodies, including that one.
  • Phones face down on the table. What happens at Rendo's ends up on Bleat anyway, but not from you.
