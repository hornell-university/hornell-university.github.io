Schedule of resident fees

At Sovereign, there are no hidden fees. Every fee is listed here, and here, and in Appendix F of your lease.

Snow removal$35 / stormBrewer Street is a hill. Hills collect snow. We remove it, eventually.
Porch couch registration$25 / monthEvery porch couch must display a current Sovereign couch tag.
Window A/C rental$40 / monthBilled year-round for administrative simplicity.
Couch elevation$45 / incidentApplies to any sofa, loveseat or futon found above the first floor exterior.
Red cup remediation$2 / cupCounted Saturday morning by our grounds partner.
Noise, anticipated$45 / weekendApplied in advance to addresses with a documented history of noise.
Key replacement$150Includes the key.
Late payment10% + relocationOn-time residents receive priority renewal. Others receive the next block down.

Fees are subject to change at the beginning of each semester, or at any other time. All fees are billed through the Resident Portal.
